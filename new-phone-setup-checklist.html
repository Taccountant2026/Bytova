PHONE SETUP · A PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

New phone: five checks before you put the old one away

Use the instructions for your actual phones and check important data, accounts and calls before retiring the old device.

Help & Community / New phone: five checks before you put the old one away

By Leighton at Bytova · Updated 9 November 2026

In this guide

  1. 1. Prepare both phones
  2. 2. Check account access
  3. 3. Follow the supported transfer path
  4. 4. Verify what arrived
  5. 5. Check service before erasing anything

1. Prepare both phones

Charge the devices and make a current backup where supported. Keep the old phone available. Check the manufacturer’s transfer instructions for your exact Android or iPhone combination.

2. Check account access

Make sure you can sign in to the accounts you need and reach their recovery options. Enter passwords and security codes yourself. Do not assume every authentication or banking app transfers automatically.

3. Follow the supported transfer path

Android setup and Apple Quick Start provide transfer options for supported devices. Read what your chosen method includes. Cross-platform transfers can have different limitations from moving between similar phones.

4. Verify what arrived

Open important photos and contacts, try the apps you rely on and check messages that matter. Read separate transfer guidance for apps such as chat services rather than assuming the device copy includes their history.

5. Check service before erasing anything

Confirm calls, mobile data and account access on the new phone. SIM or eSIM arrangements depend on your network. Agree any old-device erasure separately. Ask Bytova about one supported setup task; extensive transfers are scoped separately.
