Computer Care & Safety Check

A practical review of startup, storage, updates and common Windows security settings.

Is this the right fit?

You want to understand your computer’s everyday care and safety settings.

One clearly agreed task, with patient guidance and practical next steps. Available to home users, students, sole traders and small businesses on the same straightforward terms.

What’s included

  • Review of common startup items and available storage.
  • Review of Windows updates and common built-in security settings.
  • Agreed, appropriate settings changes where practical within the appointment.
  • Plain-English guidance on useful follow-up actions and everyday computer care.

What’s outside this appointment

  • A guarantee of faster performance, a hardware upgrade or a virus-free certificate.
  • Forensic investigation, active cyber incidents or specialist malware removal.
  • Physical hardware work and data projects; ask for a separate repair, transfer or upgrade quote.
  • An enterprise security audit or ongoing monitoring.

Before we start

  • Use a Windows computer with a working internet connection.
  • Back up important files before the appointment.
  • Tell me about any warning messages or current security concerns before booking.

A clear, realistic outcome

The price covers up to 60 minutes of the agreed remote support or review, provided with reasonable care and skill. It does not guarantee a particular fix, faster hardware or that a device is free from threats. I’ll explain what was checked and what may need further work. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
