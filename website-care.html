ONGOING HELP FOR SMALL BUSINESSES

Keep your website
useful after launch.

A small monthly check and an agreed batch of edits for one suitable website. Email Leighton first to check the platform, scope and availability.

What £49 covers

For one agreed small-business brochure website, with up to ten agreed public pages:

  • One manual monthly check of the agreed pages, navigation and visible contact details, plus the main enquiry route where a safe check is possible.
  • One batch of minor edits using the text and images you supply: for example, opening information, a price correction or a short existing-page update.
  • A short email recording what was checked, what changed and anything needing a separate decision.
  • Up to 60 minutes of work in total per service month, shared between checking, edits and the report.

The check comes first. I confirm what edits fit in the remaining time before making them. Extra work is quoted for your approval; it is not charged automatically. Unused time does not roll over.

A simple monthly process

  1. Check suitability

    Send your website address, platform if known and the kinds of changes you expect. Do not send passwords.

  2. Agree it in writing

    We confirm the website and pages, access and backup responsibilities, monthly work window, total price, payment arrangements and cancellation terms.

  3. Send one batch of changes

    Email the approved content before the agreed work window. I check the site, make the agreed edits within the allowance and email the result.

What needs a separate quote

  • New pages, redesigns, copywriting, photography and integrations.
  • Hosting, domains, email, software licences and other provider charges.
  • Ecommerce, checkout or business-critical changes, migrations and data recovery.
  • Software or plugin maintenance, backups, security monitoring, incident response and emergency repairs.

If your website needs those services, we assess them separately before any work. A visual check is not a security audit or an uptime guarantee.

Clear arrangements before you start

£49 is the total Bytova price per agreed service month; Bytova is not VAT registered. Any initial repair or setup work and third-party fees are quoted separately. You keep your own domain, hosting and other provider accounts.

Service months and availability are agreed in writing. There is no minimum multi-month commitment. Email before the next agreed service month if you want to stop; I will confirm the end date and any refund due under the agreement and applicable rights. No automatic billing is set up by this enquiry.

Contact is by email. I aim to reply within two working days; completion takes place in the agreed work window. Urgent or unlimited support is outside this offer.

Your statutory rights are unaffected. Read the service terms and ask about anything unclear before agreeing.
