Setup & Getting Started

Guided help getting one agreed software, email or supported device setup ready to use.

Is this the right fit?

You want help getting one new thing set up correctly.

One clearly agreed task, with patient guidance and practical next steps. Available to home users, students, sole traders and small businesses on the same straightforward terms.

What’s included

  • One setup task agreed before the appointment.
  • Installation or configuration of one supported app, or one email account.
  • Guided setup of one supported device, with you carrying out any physical steps.
  • A walkthrough of the important settings and how to get started.

What’s outside this appointment

  • Whole-computer or multi-device migrations and bulk data transfers.
  • Hardware assembly, cabling or hands-on installation visits.
  • Software licences, subscriptions, replacement equipment or other third-party charges.
  • Account ownership bypass, enterprise systems or ongoing administration.

Before we start

  • Email first with the app, email service or device you want to set up.
  • Have the device, account access and any required software licence ready.
  • Use a working internet connection and remain available to carry out physical steps.

A clear, realistic outcome

The price covers up to 60 minutes of the agreed remote support or review, provided with reasonable care and skill. It does not guarantee a particular fix, faster hardware or that a device is free from threats. I’ll explain what was checked and what may need further work. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
