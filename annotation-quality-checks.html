Annotation Quality Checks

Get a second pair of eyes on the labels in a batch of text, images or video.

Businesses and SMEs / Annotation Quality Checks

Is this what you need?

You already have labelled material and want to know where tags are missing, inconsistent or open to interpretation.

You receive a review sheet showing the original label, the finding and any suggested correction or question.

What we can work on

  • Check a sample against your written labelling rules.
  • Review the agreed batch for missing, misplaced or inconsistent tags.
  • Record proposed changes against the item ID or video timestamp.
  • Group repeated errors and flag examples that need a decision from you.

Useful details to bring

  • Send the label definitions and a few cleared examples.
  • State the file types, batch size and any video durations.
  • Choose a review sheet or a corrected copy of the label file.

Agreed before we begin

We agree the labels, sample or full batch, review depth and output format. Where the rules allow more than one reading, the review records the question for you.

Work is undertaken only after suitability, scope and price are accepted. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
