QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Got a question?

Here are straightforward answers before you book.

What areas do you cover?

I offer remote IT support across the whole of the UK. Where an in-person visit is possible, it is separately quoted and agreed. Get in touch to ask about your location.

How quickly can you help?

I aim to respond within two working days, although I may respond sooner when availability allows. For remote appointments, you can also check the available times directly.

Do you support home and business customers?

Yes — I work with both. Home users get straightforward, friendly support with no jargon. Businesses get practical help with their IT, websites and digital processes. Explore Home Services or Business Services.

How do I book a session?

You can book Remote Computer Help directly: 30 minutes for £25. For another service or a business enquiry, contact me with a few details and I’ll help you find the right next step.

Can every computer issue be fixed remotely?

No. Some hardware faults, outages or security incidents require other support. I’ll explain the limitations honestly and discuss the next steps.

How much is Remote Computer Help?

The 30-minute Remote Computer Help appointment is £25. PC Speed-Up is £35 for 60 minutes, and Windows Security Check is £29 for 60 minutes. Other services have separate prices or quotations.

How do I pay?

The appointment links open Calendly. Review the service, availability, price and checkout details there before confirming. A booking is confirmed only when the booking provider issues confirmation.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Review the current booking terms and the cancellation or rescheduling links in your confirmation. Your statutory rights are not affected. See the service terms for more information.

Do you offer visits?

Bytova is remote-first across the UK. Where an in-person visit is possible, it is a separately quoted and agreed service. No visit is included in a remote appointment price.
