QUESTIONS ANSWERED

A little clarity before you start.

Straightforward answers about the three services, remote access and your appointment.

How do I book and pay?

Choose your service on the booking page, then opt in to load Calendly or open its booking page in a new tab. Select an available time and follow the Stripe payment step. Check the total GBP price and your appointment details before confirming. Calendly provides the booking confirmation. You can email me if you need help.

Who is this for?

Home users, students, sole traders and small businesses. Explore home technology help or websites, marketing, IT and business systems. Routine remote sessions have fixed scope; repairs and larger projects are quoted individually.

Can you help without an internet connection?

Remote appointments need a usable computer and working internet. Physical faults can start with a repair enquiry; connection or setup work can be assessed separately. Agree suitability and arrangements before booking or sending equipment.

What if I’m not sure which service to choose?

Describe what you want to do or what’s going wrong in an email to hello@bytova.co.uk. Include your device and Windows version if known. Don’t send passwords or security codes.

Is a fix guaranteed?

No. The appointment covers the agreed support or review time, provided with reasonable care and skill. Some issues need hardware work or specialist support. I’ll explain limitations and next steps; your statutory rights remain unaffected.

Can I cancel before the appointment?

Yes. Cancel at any time before the session starts for a full refund of any appointment payment. Email me so I can arrange this. See Cancellations & Refunds for your consumer cancellation rights and what happens after work begins.

Can you start within my 14-day cancellation period?

If you are a consumer, I can start early only after you explicitly request this by email before work begins. You must also acknowledge that the cancellation right ends once the service is fully performed. Read the early-start information and use the email template there.

What does each appointment cost?

Remote Tech Help is £25 for 30 minutes. Setup & Getting Started is £35 for 60 minutes. Computer Care & Safety Check is £29 for 60 minutes. These are total appointment prices. Bytova is not VAT registered. Extra work requires a separate agreement.

Can you repair hardware or help transfer data?

Yes. Bytova offers hardware repair enquiries, upgrades and PC builds, and data transfers and backup work. These are assessed and quoted separately from routine remote appointments. Specialist recovery and failing drives need assessment first; recovery is not promised.

How do I change or cancel my appointment?

Email hello@bytova.co.uk with your name, service and appointment details. Provider booking links may also be available, but email is always an option. See Cancellations & Refunds.

Can you guarantee a faster or virus-free computer?

No. Computer Care & Safety Check is a practical review of common settings and maintenance areas. It is not a guarantee of performance, a malware investigation or a certificate that a device is free from threats.
