QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Need to know something?

Here are straightforward answers before you book.

How does remote computer support work?

Choose a service, select an available slot and follow the instructions for joining a remote session. Access is only with your consent.

Can every computer issue be fixed remotely?

No. Some hardware faults, outages or security incidents require other support. We'll explain limitations honestly.

How much is Remote Computer Help?

The 30-minute Remote Computer Help appointment is £25. Other services have separate prices or quotations.

How do I pay and book?

The Remote Computer Help link opens Calendly. Review availability, price and checkout details there. A booking is confirmed only when the provider issues confirmation.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Review the current booking terms and available cancellation or rescheduling links. Your statutory rights are not affected.

Do you offer visits?

Bytova is remote-first across the UK. Where an in-person visit is possible, it is a separately quoted and agreed service.
