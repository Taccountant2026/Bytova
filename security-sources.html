How corrections and future updates work

Collect an official advisory, verify identifiers and affected ranges, record vendor exceptions, prepare the content, run boundary and privacy tests, obtain human publication approval, then release and monitor. Source collection never automatically publishes an alert.

Data is a dated local snapshot, not a live feed. Positive patch results become unverified after the review-due date until the dataset is reviewed. A confirmed old vulnerable upstream release remains flagged. Send corrections privately by emailing Bytova; do not send exploit payloads or customer evidence.

A version result has a limited meaning.
