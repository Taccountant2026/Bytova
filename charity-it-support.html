Who can apply?

UK registered charities and UK community non-profit organisations are welcome to enquire. I may ask for your organisation's name, registration details or other evidence of non-profit status before agreeing a free session.

Priority is given to small and volunteer-led organisations with limited IT resources. This offer is managed by Bytova independently and is not a government or NCSC programme.

What is included?

  • One initial remote consultation or straightforward support session, up to 30 minutes per organisation.
  • A clear explanation of what can be checked or resolved during the session.
  • Practical written recommendations where useful.
  • Further free help may be offered individually when capacity allows, with no obligation to buy anything.

How to request support

  1. Tell me about your charity

    Send your organisation's name, a short description of the issue and how to contact you.

  2. I check suitability and availability

    I will reply in writing, ask any relevant questions and explain whether I can offer a free session.

  3. We agree the next step

    If suitable, we arrange an approved remote session. You remain in control of your device and access.

Clear boundaries, no surprise costs

The free offer is limited and subject to availability. It does not include replacement hardware, software licences, hosting, paid subscriptions, on-site visits, full website builds or ongoing managed support unless separately agreed.

It is not a 24/7 emergency response, forensic investigation or penetration-testing service. I will explain when specialist help is more appropriate. Any optional paid work would require a separate written scope and your agreement.

Do not send passwords, donor records or other sensitive personal information in your initial email. Read the privacy notice and remote support safety guidance.
