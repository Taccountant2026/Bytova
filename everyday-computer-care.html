A simple computer-care routine

Small, understandable habits for updates, files and everyday use. No miracle fixes.

Help & Community / A simple computer-care routine

By Leighton at Bytova · Updated 19 October 2026

In this guide

  1. Know where your important files live
  2. Review updates thoughtfully
  3. Understand storage and startup
  4. Keep a short record
  5. Ask before making a change you are unsure about

Know where your important files live

Identify the documents and photographs you would miss. Keep a separate backup and check that a sample can be restored. Understand what your chosen backup includes; synchronising an account does not automatically mean every file on the computer is protected.

Review updates thoughtfully

Use the update facilities supplied with your operating system and trusted apps. Read what a restart or change involves, save your work and allow time for it. Check that your operating system is still supported; a familiar-looking desktop is not proof of ongoing security support.

Understand storage and startup

Review available storage and apps that start with the computer. Remove something only when you understand what it is and no longer need it. Do not delete unfamiliar system files or install a cleaner because an advert promises an instant speed boost.

Keep a short record

Note recurring errors, unusual changes and the last backup check. This makes it easier to explain an issue later. Avoid keeping passwords or security answers in a support note or sharing them in a community tip.

Ask before making a change you are unsure about
