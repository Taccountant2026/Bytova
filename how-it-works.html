How remote IT support works

Practical support from the comfort of your home or office, without having to take your computer anywhere.

  1. Choose the right service

    Select quick remote help, a PC speed-up or a Windows security check. We explain what each service covers before you book.

  2. Pick a convenient appointment

    Choose an available UK-time appointment through Calendly and review the full price and cancellation terms before paying.

  3. Join securely

    Follow the appointment instructions to connect with your technician. Only use a remote-access tool you recognise and have agreed to install. You remain in control of your computer.

  4. Get a clear outcome

    We'll explain what was checked, any changes made, and recommended next steps. Some issues need hardware repair or an additional appointment.

Your security matters

Never share your banking password, one-time sign-in codes or full payment card information. You can stop a remote support session at any time. Back up important files before major system changes.
