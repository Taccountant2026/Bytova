HOW IT WORKS

Clear from your first question.

Know what’s included, how to prepare and what happens during your remote appointment.

  1. Choose the right service

    Read what’s included and tell me the one task or issue. If you’re unsure whether remote help is suitable, email first.

  2. Choose a time and pay

    Select an available time in Calendly. Check your service, total GBP price, time zone and cancellation information before paying. Your appointment is confirmed when Calendly issues its confirmation. Email me if you need help booking.

  3. Connect with me

    Joining instructions are emailed before your appointment. Stay at your device and only accept the connection you are expecting.

  4. Understand the next steps

    I’ll explain what was checked or changed and any practical follow-up. A remote appointment cannot guarantee that every problem will be resolved.

Before your appointment

  • Back up important files before any work begins.
  • Have a working internet connection, access to your device and permission to make changes.
  • Keep any required account access available, but enter passwords yourself and never send them to me.
  • If you need an appointment within your consumer cancellation period, send the early-start request before work begins.

Your remote connection

Bytova uses AnyDesk for remote support. Joining instructions are emailed before your appointment. Only accept a connection you are expecting from me, stay present and close personal banking or other sensitive windows.

You can disconnect or withdraw access at any time. AnyDesk access is not a guarantee that a device is safe or that an issue can be resolved.
