Privacy information

Bytova provides remote IT support. This preview website is hosted as a static site and does not itself collect payment card details or operate a customer account database.

Bookings and payments

When you choose to book, you leave this site for Calendly. Calendly processes booking details such as your name, email address and appointment selection. Payments are processed through Stripe. Please review their privacy notices during booking. Bytova uses appointment information to arrange and deliver the requested service, respond to enquiries and keep appropriate business records.

Website hosting

The website host may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser requests to serve pages and maintain security. This preview does not use its own analytics or advertising cookies.

Your rights and contact

UK data protection rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection, depending on the circumstances. To raise a privacy enquiry, use the verified contact details on Bytova's existing website. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Before production launch: the business controller identity, direct privacy contact, retention periods and lawful-basis details require verification and publication. This is a provisional notice, not a claim of completed legal compliance.

Bytova provides remote support only. In-person visits are not offered.
