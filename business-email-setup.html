All Services / Business Email Setup

Is this what you need?

You want a professional email address or help configuring a supported business mailbox.

An agreed mailbox setup and a clear handover, with provider subscriptions owned by you.

What we can work on

  • Review the existing domain and mailbox setup.
  • Agree one mailbox or a separately scoped multi-user project.
  • Configure supported devices and check sending and receiving within the agreed scope.
  • Explain ownership, recovery and ongoing provider charges.

Useful details to bring

  • Tell us your domain and email provider.
  • State how many accounts and devices need help.
  • Explain any existing mail or migration needs.
  • Keep passwords and recovery codes private.

Agreed before we begin

For one suitable setup task, the £35 / 60-minute Setup appointment may fit after review. Domains, mailbox subscriptions and licences are additional. DNS changes, migrations, multiple users and provider administration are quoted separately before work.

Work is undertaken only after suitability, scope and price are accepted. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
