  1. Tell me the result you need

    Start with a device fault, a business problem or a project idea. A short brief is enough to begin.

  2. Agree a sensible scope

    I’ll check suitability and explain the work, price, timescale, responsibilities and any additional costs.

  3. Approve the work

    You decide whether to proceed. Any diagnostic charge, parts purchase, handover or access is agreed beforehand.

  4. Receive a clear handover

    We check the agreed result, cover anything outstanding and make the next steps understandable.

Quotes identify deliverables, responsibilities, revisions or tests, third-party costs and the handover. Larger changes are agreed before they are added.
