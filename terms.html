3. Suitability and appointment confirmation

Tell me accurately what help you need before confirming. You need a working internet connection, access to the device and permission to make the changes involved. You must be able to remain present and follow reasonable joining instructions. A printer must already be powered on, connected and reachable.

A service contract is agreed when I accept your appointment or the booking provider issues its confirmation for the stated service, price and time. I will provide the agreed scope and arrangements by email, which you should keep. Do not assume a message or selected time alone confirms an appointment.

Book through Calendly by choosing an available time and completing the Stripe payment step. Check the service, total GBP price and appointment details before confirming. If you need help choosing or booking, email me before proceeding. This website does not collect card details.

4. What the appointment can achieve

I will provide the agreed service with reasonable care and skill. The appointment price covers the agreed support, setup guidance or review time, up to the stated duration. Some issues cannot be resolved remotely or within that time. I will explain practical limitations and next steps.

These services do not guarantee a fix, faster hardware, recovery of data, or that a device is free from viruses or other threats. They do not cover hardware repair, migration, account ownership bypass, active fraud or cyber incident response, specialist malware investigation, enterprise administration or ongoing managed IT.

5. Your device, data and remote access

Back up important files before work begins and tell me if you cannot do this. You remain responsible for giving accurate instructions and having the rights and licences needed for your device and software. Keep credentials private and enter your own passwords when required.

Remote access takes place only with your permission. Stay present, close sensitive windows and only accept the expected connection. You can ask to stop or disconnect at any time. Read Remote Support Safety before the appointment.

6. Cancellation and early starts

You may cancel at any time before the session starts for a full refund of any appointment payment. Consumers also generally have a 14-day cancellation period starting the day after the service contract is made. An appointment within that period starts only after your explicit early-start request by email, with the required acknowledgement about a fully performed service.

Read Cancellations & Refunds for the full policy, the model cancellation form and the early-start email. Those terms form part of the service agreement.

Nothing here excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including liability for fraud, or death or personal injury caused by negligence. Any responsibility for a problem depends on the circumstances and applicable law. Your statutory rights remain protected.
