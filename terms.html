Service terms

Provisional customer information: these terms require business-owner review before the replacement website becomes the primary live website. Your statutory rights are unaffected.

Remote-first services

Bytova predominantly delivers services remotely. If a problem requires physical attendance, an on-site call-out may be offered as a separate product subject to location, availability, an agreed scope and a separate charge. No call-out is included in a remote appointment price, and no visit will be arranged without a quotation and customer approval. We do not guarantee every issue can be resolved.

Services and prices

Remote Computer Help costs £25 for 30 minutes; PC Speed-Up costs £35 for 60 minutes; Windows Security Check costs £29 for 60 minutes. Business IT Support starts from £49 and requires a separate quote. Additional work requires your agreement.

Booking and payment

Fixed-price appointments are booked using Calendly and paid through Stripe. Check the service, time, total price and cancellation information before paying. A booking is confirmed only when you receive confirmation from Calendly.

Remote access and safety

Remote access is used only with your consent. Keep backups of important files and do not share banking passwords, one-time security codes or full card details. You can ask to stop remote access at any time.

Cancellation and refunds

UK consumer cancellation rights depend on the circumstances, including when you book and whether you expressly request services to begin during the statutory cancellation period. Nothing on this page removes statutory rights. Review the applicable cancellation terms before checkout and contact Bytova promptly if you need to cancel or complain. Do not assume an appointment is non-refundable solely because it was booked online.

Contact and complaints

For business identity, verified contact details and enquiries, see the existing Bytova website. The replacement site's final legal identity and direct complaints contact details must be verified before primary launch.
