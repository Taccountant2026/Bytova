All Services / Networks, NAS & Storage Setup

Is this what you need?

You need shared storage, a home server, a NAS or a practical small-office network configuration.

A documented setup with agreed permissions, checks and backup responsibilities.

What we can work on

  • Supported NAS and shared-storage setup.
  • Home and small-office LAN, Wi-Fi and guest-network configuration.
  • Practical access, sharing and backup arrangements.
  • A handover covering the agreed configuration and maintenance tasks.

Useful details to bring

  • List the equipment and operating systems.
  • Describe who needs access and from where.
  • Explain what data is held and its importance.
  • Identify the account owner and current backup arrangements.

Agreed before we begin

Existing systems, business impact and supported equipment are assessed first. Business-critical migrations, high-availability infrastructure and ongoing monitoring need a specifically agreed scope; this is not a 24-hour managed service.

Work is undertaken only after suitability, scope and price are accepted. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
