All Services / PC Upgrades & Custom Builds

Is this what you need?

You want more usable storage, a sensible upgrade or a desktop for everyday work, gaming or creating.

A compatible specification with a clear parts-and-labour quote and agreed testing.

What we can work on

  • SSD, NVMe and RAM upgrade assessment and fitting.
  • Suitable CPU, motherboard, graphics card and power-supply work.
  • New PC build planning, assembly and configuration.
  • Cleaning, cooling and practical maintenance agreed for the device.

Useful details to bring

  • List the current model or main components.
  • Describe what you use the computer for.
  • Set a total budget including parts, if known.
  • Say whether existing files need transferring.

Agreed before we begin

Compatibility and the condition of existing equipment are checked before agreeing a specification. Parts, labour, licences, data work and any customer-supplied components are itemised. Performance depends on the complete system.

Work is undertaken only after suitability, scope and price are accepted. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
