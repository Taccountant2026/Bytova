Individuals and households / Computer & Hardware Repairs

Is this what you need?

A device will not start, has a damaged part, overheats or is no longer working as it should.

Understand the fault, the viable repair and the agreed cost before authorising work.

What we can work on

  • PC and laptop hardware fault finding and repair.
  • Component replacement and supported device repairs, subject to model and parts availability.
  • Console hardware enquiries assessed by device and fault.
  • An explanation of the work, checks performed and any unresolved limitations.

Useful details to bring

  • Note the device make and model.
  • Describe the fault and when it started.
  • Tell Leighton about earlier repairs, damage or liquid exposure.
  • Discuss backup needs before any repair or handover.

Agreed before we begin

Diagnostics, labour, parts, testing and any return arrangements are agreed in writing. Any diagnostic charge is stated before you authorise it. Do not send or drop off equipment until Leighton confirms the arrangements.

Work is undertaken only after suitability, scope and price are accepted. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
