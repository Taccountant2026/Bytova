New laptop: check your files before retiring the old one

Plan the transfer, verify what arrived and keep a separate copy before erasing or giving away the old computer.

Help & Community / New laptop: check your files before retiring the old one

By Leighton at Bytova · Updated 9 November 2026

In this guide

  1. Make an inventory first
  2. Choose and check the transfer method
  3. Open files on the new computer
  4. Keep a recovery route

Make an inventory first

List the folders, photos, email accounts and apps you actually use. Check Downloads and any folders outside Documents. A shortcut or familiar filename is not proof that the underlying file has been copied.

Choose and check the transfer method

Use a suitable external-drive copy or the supported backup route for your accounts. Windows Backup uses a personal Microsoft account and selected OneDrive folders; work accounts, local-only data and some apps need separate arrangements. Check storage capacity and completion messages.

Open files on the new computer

Try several important documents and photos from the destination. Check account access and app licences separately. A successful file copy does not prove every app, setting or mailbox has migrated.

Keep a recovery route

Retain the old computer and an independent copy until the required files and accounts are checked. Agree any erasure or disposal separately. Bytova’s £35 Setup appointment covers one agreed supported task; whole-computer migrations and bulk transfers need a separate written quote.
