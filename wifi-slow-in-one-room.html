Why is Wi-Fi slow in one room?

Compare the connection in two places before changing settings or buying equipment.

Help & Community / Why is Wi-Fi slow in one room?

By Leighton at Bytova · Updated 9 November 2026

In this guide

  1. Compare the same device in two places
  2. Separate a coverage problem from a wider problem
  3. Make one small change
  4. Choose the next step from the evidence

Compare the same device in two places

Try the same ordinary task near the router, then in the problem room. Note the time and result. A single speed test is a snapshot; repeat the comparison before drawing a conclusion.

Separate a coverage problem from a wider problem

If only the distant room struggles, walls, distance or interference may matter. If every room struggles, check another device and your provider’s service status. A weak signal and a slow internet connection need different checks.

Make one small change

Where practical, try the router in a more open, central position, away from large metal obstructions. Compare again. Keep the existing working setup available; do not reset the router or change security settings to test coverage.

Choose the next step from the evidence

An extender, mesh point or wired access point may help some layouts, but placement and the link back to the router matter. Send Bytova the router model, affected rooms and comparison results before arranging suitable help. Equipment and network projects are quoted separately.
