The free business-check.html tool gives up to three starting priorities and carries those general selections into an enquiry. It does not require an email address, submit answers or save device data.

The project-enquiry.html page still builds a reviewable email draft. It now also offers explicit private submission with a reply name, email and privacy acknowledgement. It uses https://retroreplay.uk/api/leads, operated by the same controller, and saves the enquiry in the existing protected business system. Only the two exact Bytova HTTPS origins and the enquiry service’s own origin are accepted. Browser credentials are omitted. Stable request keys prevent duplicate retries. Copying or building sends nothing.

Owner inbox: https://retroreplay.uk/admin#funnels. The existing owner sign-in applies. Filter Bytova, review a request, prepare a personal reply and record the stage, next action and follow-up date. No customer auto-reply or marketing subscription is created. Existing enabled owner alerts use the saved RRUK email connection; no new sender setup is required for private owner notifications. Provider acceptance and inbox delivery remain distinct.

A short utm_campaign label (letters, digits, spaces, dots, underscores and hyphens, up to 60 characters) can travel in local links and be included with an explicit enquiry. It uses URL parameters only: no analytics cookies, device profile, browsing log or visitor conversion denominator is added. Remote Calendly booking outcomes are not reported to this inbox.

The three retained paid remote appointments and launch gates remain separate. Quoted work never acquires a remote appointment price. Personal replies, quotes and follow-ups must be reviewed and sent by the owner. A due date is an inbox queue, not an automatic message or scheduled reminder.
