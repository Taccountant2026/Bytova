How do I book and pay?

Choose your service on the booking page, then opt in to load Calendly or open its booking page in a new tab. Select an available time and follow the Stripe payment step. Check the total GBP price and your appointment details before confirming. Calendly provides the booking confirmation. You can email me if you need help.

Who is this for?

Home users, students, sole traders and small businesses who need one straightforward task or issue addressed. The same clear scope applies to everyone; this is not enterprise IT support.

Can you help without an internet connection?

You need a working internet connection and must be able to use the device and join a remote session. A broken internet connection, hardware failure or unreachable printer cannot be repaired through this remote service. Email first if you’re unsure.

Not sure what you need?

Tell me the one task or issue and I’ll help you decide whether a remote appointment is suitable.
