What areas do you cover?

I offer remote IT support across the whole of the UK. Where an in-person visit is possible, it is separately quoted and agreed. Get in touch to ask about your location.

How quickly can you help?

I aim to respond within two working days, although I may respond sooner when availability allows. For remote appointments, you can also check the available times directly.

Do you support home and business customers?

Yes — I work with both. Home users get straightforward, friendly support with no jargon. Businesses get practical help with their IT, websites and digital processes. Explore Home Services or Business Services.

How do I book a session?

You can book Remote Computer Help directly: 30 minutes for £25. For another service or a business enquiry, contact me with a few details and I’ll help you find the right next step.
